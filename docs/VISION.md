# Vision

Odyva turns fresh hiring intent into a relevant, explainable, and approval-gated GTM action.

For the hackathon, the product promise is intentionally narrow:

~~~text
find a recent Greenhouse job
  ↓
understand the company's likely need
  ↓
compare it to verified capabilities
  ↓
explain the fit
  ↓
draft personalized outreach
  ↓
get human approval
  ↓
send or dry-run the email
  ↓
save what happened
~~~

The value is the closed loop, not the number of integrations.

## Killer demo moment

~~~text
This job was posted 11 hours ago.
Odyva found a 92% capability match.
Here is exactly why.
Here is the outreach.
Approve.
Email sent.
~~~

The recommendation must be grounded in the job posting and capability profile.

The production vision may eventually include continuous discovery, durable workflows, richer memory, more sources, and cloud infrastructure. Those are future extensions. The hackathon proves only the smallest valuable loop.
